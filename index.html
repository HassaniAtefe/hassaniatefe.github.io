I'm a Ph.D. student at the University of Manchester, advised by Dr. Mobarak Hoque, Prof. Tim Cootes, and Ashwin Sachdeva. My Ph.D. research focuses on Multimodal LLM Agents in Healthcare. More broadly, I build multimodal LLM-based systems for healthcare, with a focus on medical imaging, multimodal reasoning, and trustworthy AI. My research explores how multimodal agents can integrate heterogeneous medical information and reason across different modalities to tackle complex healthcare tasks and support reliable clinical decision-making.

Before Manchester, I was a researcher at King's College London, the University of Exeter, and Imperial College London, working on fairness, multimodal LVLMs, and federated learning for medical imaging.
